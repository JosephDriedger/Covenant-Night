COVENANT NIGHT — Credits

Design, code, sound design and illustration: a one-person project.
Scriptural basis: 1 Samuel 19-20. The epilogue quotes 1 Samuel 20:42.

Asset sources
-------------
All characters, environment geometry, textures, particle effects, sound effects, music and story illustrations
in this build are original and generated procedurally by the project's editor tools
(Covenant_Night/Assets/Editor/Builder). No third-party art or audio is included.

Engine and packages
-------------------
Unity 6 (Universal Render Pipeline), Input System, AI Navigation, TextMeshPro (Unity Technologies).
