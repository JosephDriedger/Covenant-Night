# Store Page Draft

Copy for a store listing (itch.io, Steam, etc.). Screenshots referenced below are in `docs/screenshots`.

## Title

Covenant Night

## Short description / tagline

A third-person 3D stealth game of loyalty, shadow, and sacrifice. Escort David past your father's guards before dawn.

## Long description

Play as Jonathan, son of King Saul, and escort your closest friend David through the winding streets of ancient Gibeah before your father's guards close every exit.

Hug walls, throw stones, drop decoys, command your companion, and navigate a city that knows your face. Every shadow is cover. Every patrol route is a puzzle. Every choice carries the weight of a covenant sworn before God.

No combat. Movement, patience, and loyalty are the only tools.

**Five hand-built zones**, each a self-contained stealth puzzle with at least two routes through — a fast way and a safer one.

**A companion, not a follower.** David has his own detection rules, his own commands (Follow, Wait, Run, Hush, Harp), and his own risk: a confirmed sighting of him is instant trouble.

**Guards that read at a glance.** Green, amber, and red detection cones tell you exactly what a guard knows before it's too late.

**Four difficulties plus New Game+.** Easy through Hardcore (one life, smarter guards), and after your first win, a Mixed mode that lets you pick a difficulty for each zone individually.

**Two endings.** How you reach the eastern gate decides which one you get.

**A living, procedural world.** Every character, texture, illustration, and sound in this build is original, generated by the game's own tools. The only outside assets are two open-licensed typefaces.

## Key features

- Third-person stealth with no combat — crouch, throw, Shadow Step, and a droppable decoy
- Command an AI companion with his own stealth rules and abilities
- Five zones, each with multiple routes, including a rooftop shortcut in two of them
- Guard AI with a real finite-state machine: Unaware, Suspicious, Alarmed
- Cinematic intro, zone transitions, and ending — staged with live camera work and character animation, not slideshows
- Four difficulty modes plus a New-Game+ Mixed mode
- Two distinct endings
- Full gamepad support with on-screen prompts that follow whichever device you're using
- Local best-time tracking per zone
- Optional lore scrolls for players who want to read between the lines

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Xbox | PlayStation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move / look | WASD / mouse | Left stick / right stick | Left stick / right stick |
| Sneak (silent) | Left Shift (hold) | B (hold) | Circle (hold) |
| Sprint (loud) | Left Ctrl or Cmd (hold) | Click left stick (LS) | L3 |
| Shadow Step | Space (hold, beside a wall) | A (hold) | Cross (hold) |
| Throw a stone | Left mouse | X | Square |
| Drop a decoy | G | RB | R1 |
| David: Follow / Wait / Run | 1 / 2 / 3 | D-pad left / right / down | D-pad left / right / down |
| Hush David | Q | LB | L1 |
| Harp Calm | H | D-pad up | D-pad up |
| Pause | Esc | Menu | Options |
